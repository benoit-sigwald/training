AI literacy obligation

Article 4 of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) requires organisations deploying an AI system to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy among their staff. The obligation has applied since 2 February 2025, with no size threshold and regardless of risk level — using a conversational assistant is enough to trigger it.

National authorities can enforce and fine from 2 August 2026. This programme and its attendance sheet are the documentary evidence of that training.

The CRAFT matrix.

Five ingredients. It is the core of module 02, and the only model worth memorising.

CContext

The situation, the "why". A company that is growing and one in crisis do not get the same answer.

RRole

Who the tool should be. "You are an experienced HR manager" sets the level of expertise.

AAction

The expected deliverable, with a clear verb: write, summarise, analyse, compare.

FFormat

The shape of the output: email, table, six slides. Without it, the tool decides for you.

TTone

The register: professional, warm, direct. This is what makes the text ring true.

The slash, two natures.

The core of module 00. Not everything that starts with "/" is the same kind of thing.

1Built-in command

Fixed logic, hard-coded in the CLI. It does one precise thing, with no reasoning from the model, and cannot be customised. /clear, /compact, /model, /cost, /help.

2Skill

A prompt-based capability: invoking it loads a Markdown instruction file into the context, which Claude then executes — with arguments, dedicated tools and possible subagents. .claude/commands/ still works, .claude/skills/ is the recommended approach.

Three environments, three behaviours

Claude CodeThe full palette: context (/clear, /compact, /context), navigation (/resume, /branch, /rewind), model (/model, /effort), code (/plan, /diff, /security-review).
ConnectorsA connected MCP server exposes its prompts as /mcp__server__prompt. Plugins expose theirs as /plugin:command.
claude.aiA much smaller palette: /new clears the context, /help lists what exists. Everything else after a "/" is either the name of a skill, or plain text.

Two distinctions that cost time: /cd moves the session — a later /resume finds it from the new directory — whereas /add-dir grants access without moving, which is what you want in a monorepo. And /branch switches to a copy of the conversation while preserving the original, which you return to with /resume.

Validation by evidence.

No quiz. A deliverable, a number, a certificate.

1

Baseline. Module 00: three recurring tasks, actually timed, before anything else. Without that starting point, the "time saved" at the end is just a feeling.

2

Attendance sheet. One per session, signed by participants and facilitator. That is the document asked for in an audit.

3

Final demo. Module 11: before, after, time saved, in figures. The real deliverable is required, not the conversation.

4

Certificate of completion. Issued on demo day, listing modules attended, dates and measured gain. Template in docs/attestation.md.
